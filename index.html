— ABOUT ME

I started as a developer.
I stayed for design.

Got my BSc in Computer Science from SKBU University, but somewhere between building frontend interfaces with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React Js, I realized I was most alive figuring out why the button should go there, not how to code it.

Self-taught through late nights, webinars, and a lot of trial and error. Then an internship. Then a real design role at The Sales Studio, shipping UI for a PWA, SaaS platform, and content product.

I'm not just a designer who thinks in pixels. I think in systems, in code, in how real users interact when they're tired or distracted.

Outside of design: I draw, doodle, cycle, chase mountains, obsessively notice how flowers are designed by nature.

I'm building Kryzen, my personal design brand, a creative space where design, storytelling, and innovation come together to create meaningful digital experiences.
